## The north star (from Yev's answers)

> Q2 write-in: *"I want the features to be optional and for it to be fun to fly around."* · Q4 write-in: *"have an option for unlimited travel."*

**Flying is the game. Everything else is optional garnish.** The slice succeeds if drifting through gravity with a camera *feels good on its own* — before any mission, economy, or collectible. So the build order is: nail the flight-and-camera feel first, prove performance, close one "go somewhere and come back" loop, then layer optional richness that a player can ignore.

## Architecture principles (so the slice doesn't paint us into a corner)

- **Explore is a new module, golf is untouched.** Add `explore.js` (world/camera/streaming/mode state) that **reuses `physics.js` wholesale** (`gravityAt`/`stepBody`/`collide`/`orbitCapture` are already pure and body-list-driven). `gameplay.js` stays the golf brain. `state.js` gains an explore sub-state. This keeps the 241 golf tests green and isolates risk.
- **Two view transforms.** Golf keeps the fixed 100×170 letterbox (`ui.js` `view`). Explore uses a **camera transform** centered on the comet. `toWorld`/render branch on mode. Don't retrofit the letterbox into a camera — add a parallel path.
- **Gravity from an active set, not all bodies.** `gravityAt` currently sums every body — fine for ~5 golf bodies, fatal for a populated sector. Explore feeds `stepBody` a **culled near-comet body list** (spatial buckets by chunk). This is the perf keystone.
- **Deterministic seeded generation.** A small hash-based PRNG keyed on `(chunkX, chunkY, worldSeed)` so any coordinate regenerates identically → revisiting a place is stable and the home bookmark actually points somewhere. (Durak Dungeon's seeded-run pattern is the in-repo reference.)
- **Everything optional.** Collectibles, later missions, energy toggles — all skippable; flying with zero objectives must be complete on its own.

## Sprint 1 — Vertical Slice (the next iteration) — agent-shippable

Priorities: **P0** foundational blocker, **P1** slice-critical. Recommended build order OW-1 → OW-2 → OW-3 → OW-4; OW-1/2 are the risky architecture, OW-3 closes the loop, OW-4 adds the first reward. Each is its own commit; ship and verify per `dev-ship`.

- [x] **P0 · OW-1 — 🌌 Explore mode + camera + unlimited free-fling.** New Explore option on the start screen; enter a camera-followed world (comet-centered, smooth-eased, parallax starfield) far bigger than one screen. Flinging works in any direction with the existing drag aim; **no cup pressure, no strokes/par** — golf HUD swapped for a minimal explore HUD. Reuses `physics.js`. Golf modes and their 241 tests untouched. *Done when:* pick 🌌 Explore → fling freely around a space larger than the viewport with a smooth-follow camera and no golf HUD; Endless/Round still play identically; verified on phone-size + desktop with no console errors; unit tests for the camera world↔screen transform.
- [x] **P1 · OW-2 — Seeded sector generation + spatial culling.** Populate a bounded sector from a **coordinate-seeded PRNG** with varied bodies (planets + **star variety**: giants/dwarfs/binary pairs — just mass/radius params), deterministic on revisit. Only bodies near the camera exert gravity and draw (chunk-bucketed active set); distant chunks sleep/unload. *Done when:* the sector is populated and the same `worldSeed` regenerates byte-identical body layout; a headless perf check confirms off-screen bodies are excluded from `gravityAt`/render; frame rate holds with a full sector; pure generation + culling logic unit-tested (determinism + "only near-camera bodies active").
- [ ] **P1 · OW-3 — Black-hole wormhole → town → return portal (the loop).** ⏩ **Not built in Sprint 1 — Town arrived a different way (landing on the tee rock, EXP-1) and functionally superseded this for a while.** Next Arc Questionnaire Q2 (2026-07-17) confirmed Yev still wants the wormhole-dive entrance built, **additive** to the tee rock. Moved into **Sprint 2, Wave 1** below — full re-spec there accounts for the fact Town/the Shop didn't exist yet when this was first written.
- [ ] **P1 · OW-4 — Stardust pickups (the first optional reward).** Scatter collectible **stardust** through the sector (seeded placement); flying near one collects it with a little burst + sound; a running session total persists (`blackHoleInOne_stardust`). No spend yet (town shop is Sprint 3) — purely "nice to grab, fine to ignore," honoring "features optional." *Done when:* stardust appears in the sector, is collected on proximity with feedback, the total persists and shows on the explore HUD, and a run with zero pickups is still complete; collection logic unit-tested; key in `clearAllGameData`.

## Sprint 2 — Richness — planned 2026-07-17, Wave 1 ready to ship

Consumes [Next Arc Questionnaire](../../../archive/questionnaires/black-hole-in-one-next-arc-july-2026.md) Q2/Q3 plus three follow-up calls made in the planning chat the same day. **North star unchanged: flying is the game, everything here is optional garnish** — none of Sprint 2 gates progress, none of it adds power creep (Q7's "cosmetics only" precedent extends to all of it).

**Decisions consumed:**
- **Q1 (flying vs. flicking verdict):** *both have a place; items modify gameplay; flicking (Thruster off) stays the default.* No code change from this alone — it's a confirmation, not a build item.
- **Q2 (arc pick):** Open World Sprint 2 (Richness), plus **OW-3's wormhole-dive Town entrance is still wanted**, additive to the tee rock — not a replacement.
- **Q3 (Shop Options Bleed bug):** bundle into this arc rather than a standalone dev-fix — it's the sprint's first item below.
- **Drifters** (one of the original Q6 encounter picks): **parked, not scoped this sprint** — no design exists for what they should be (ambient debris vs. landable roamers vs. something else), and nothing else in Sprint 2 depends on them. Revisit after Wave 1/2 ship if the sector still feels static once moons/rings/wormhole-pairs/derelicts are in. Logged in Improvements.md's Parked section.
- **Moons & rings scope:** **decorative only** — no gravity, no landing, no collision. This reopens the previously-parked "moving bodies" idea (Improvements.md's Parked/declined section), but safely: that idea was cut because golf's "everything frozen at rest" pause-proofing pillar couldn't tolerate idle motion. Golf is untouched here — Explore already has continuous motion (OW-0 mid-flight pushes, the Thruster) and Yev's July 15 ruling that live motion is fine in Explore. Interactive moons (gravity + landable) stayed on the table as an option but decorative-only was the pick — a real orbiting-gravity-body version can always be a later sprint if decorative reads as flat.
- **Execution model:** Yev is running one Claude Code session per item, **sequentially on `main`** (not parallel worktrees) — no merge-conflict risk, but it does mean each session should `git pull` before starting and re-run the full suite (not just its own new tests) before committing, since later items build on earlier ones landing clean.

**Wave split:** Wave 1 is the foundational/architecturally-novel items — ship these first, then **Yev plays and confirms the richness direction lands before Wave 2 starts.** This mirrors how Sprint 1 itself was verdict-gated. There's no formal questionnaire gate here since Yev is driving the sessions directly — Wave 2 items just shouldn't be picked up until he's said Wave 1 feels good.

### Wave 1 (ship first, then checkpoint)

- [x] **P0 · Bug — Shop Options Bleed.** ✅ **Shipped 2026-07-17 (`185f903`)** — see [Improvements](../ideas.md)'s Shipped section for the full root cause and verification trail (checklist there is this build plan's source of truth for what's done, per the convention below). The known live bug from the Core Re-alignment cleanup: `ui.showHowto()` (`ui.js:699`) hides `#bar`/`#exploreBar`/`#editorBar`/`#customBar` but never `#townShop`, so opening the ☰ menu while the Town Shop panel is open in Explore leaves it visibly bled over the menu. **The spec'd one-line fix (add `#townShop` to `showHowto()`'s hide list) turned out incomplete** — `updateTownShop()` re-derives visibility from `atTown()` every render frame and would silently re-show the shop one frame later. Actual fix needed a `menuOpen` flag gating `updateTownShop()`'s own visibility computation. *Done when:* opening the menu while the Town Shop is open fully hides the shop panel, in both directions (open menu → shop hidden; close menu while still at Town → shop panel correctly reappears); verified in-browser at 375px and desktop widths; zero other UI regressions.

- [x] **P0 · OW-3 — Black-hole wormhole → Town → return portal (additive second entrance).** ✅ **Shipped 2026-07-17 (`8ad331c`)** — the recommended approach below held up against the current code (checked before building, per this doc's own instruction); see [Improvements](../ideas.md)'s Shipped section for the full trail. **Key fact for whoever picks this up: Explore currently has zero black holes.** `world.blackHole` is hardcoded `null` in `explore.js`'s `startRun()` — black holes only exist in golf (`gameplay.js`/`editor.js`), where `physics.js`'s `gravityAt`/`stepBody` take a single dedicated `blackHole` param (not part of the general `bodies` array) with its own capture-radius sink check. Town today is reached only by landing the comet on the tee rock at `(50, 85)` (EXP-1, shipped, works fine, **must stay working unchanged**).
  - **Recommended approach (confirm against current code before committing, don't take this as gospel — re-verify file:line since the codebase moves fast):** seed one or a few black holes as sector content — a new body flavor in `getChunkBodies()` alongside giant/dwarf/binary/normal, rare enough to read as a landmark rather than clutter. Rather than retrofitting `physics.js`'s single-slot `blackHole` param (shared with golf, backing ~300 tests — avoid touching its signature if at all possible), give `explore.step()` its own proximity check against active-chunk bodies flagged `type: 'blackhole'`, driving Explore's own warp-to-Town transition independent of golf's `beginSink`/`stepSink`/`holeComplete` (those are strokes/par scoring functions, meaningless in Explore) — reuse the *visual pattern* of the inward spiral (`gameplay.js`'s `stepSink`), not the functions themselves.
  - On warp: teleport to Town (the existing tee rock), remembering which black hole (id + world coords) sent you there.
  - At Town: a **🌀 Return Portal** (a new affordance near the tee rock, or folded into the existing Town Shop bottom-sheet chrome) sends you back to that remembered black hole's exact location. Persist the bookmark + `worldSeed` (`blackHoleInOne_exploreHome`) so it survives reload, per the original OW-3 spec.
  - *Done when:* flying to a seeded black hole and getting close warps to Town with a distinct transition; the Return Portal sends you back to the exact black hole + location you came from; the bookmark persists across reload; landing on the tee rock directly still works completely unchanged and the Town Shop still only offers there (`atTown()` untouched); golf modes (Endless, Editor) are **byte-identical** — the full existing suite (300+ tests) stays green with zero new failures; new placement/warp/return logic is unit tested; new keys registered in `clearAllGameData`.
  - **OW-3 open sub-decisions (defaulted, non-blocking — revisit after playing, same convention as Sprint 1's):**
    - **Rarity/size** — landmark rate defaulted to ~1 in 25 chunks, fixed radius 22 (visually large, between a Dwarf and a Giant). Tune after flying around — could read as too rare or too common depending on how Sensor-upgrade chunk range feels in practice.
    - **Return Portal cost** — free (no fuel/stardust charge), since it reads as "returning to where you were" rather than a flight action. Could instead cost fuel or a small stardust fee if it feels too free relative to the Town Shop's other stardust sinks.
    - **Return Portal placement** — folded into the Town Shop bottom-sheet (per the "or folded into..." option above) rather than a separate affordance near the tee rock, since it reuses the shop's existing show/hide plumbing. A dedicated on-map affordance is still on the table if the shop panel feels like the wrong home for it.
    - **Landing velocity after the Return Portal** — resets to zero (floating, not carrying momentum) rather than preserving the velocity the comet had when it warped in. Zero felt safer (no chance of flying straight back into the black hole on arrival) but hasn't been played against the alternative.

- [x] **P1 · OW-5 — Moons & rings (decorative).** ✅ **Shipped 2026-07-17 (`28a6990`)** — see [Improvements](../ideas.md)'s Shipped section for full details. A moon or ring is now a property on an existing Giant planet body (`b.moon`/`b.ring`), never a separate entry in `world.bodies` — structurally incapable of gravity/collision by construction, not just by testing. Moon position is a pure function of the cosmetic `S.time` clock (fixed orbit radius/period); ring is a static painted arc. Scoped to Giants only (spec's "natural candidate") rather than all planet types — see the open sub-decision below. *Done when:* moons visibly orbit over time and rings render statically; a comet can fly straight through either with zero physics interaction, confirmed by checking they never enter the gravity-bearing body list (not just "looks fine visually"); golf untouched; placement/orbit-math is a pure function, unit tested for determinism.
  - **OW-5 open sub-decisions (defaulted, non-blocking — revisit after playing, same convention as OW-3's):**
    - **Giants-only scope** — the spec said "a meaningful share of Explore's planets — giants are the natural candidate," which could be read either as "giants only" or "mostly giants, occasionally others." Defaulted to giants-only (~65% of giants get one) since dwarfs/normals are small enough that a moon/ring would likely read as visual clutter rather than richness. Could extend to normal-sized planets at a lower rate if giants alone feel too sparse in practice.
    - **Moon-vs-ring split and tuning constants** — 50/50 moon-vs-ring, moon orbit period 14–22s, orbit radius 1.6–2.4× planet radius, ring radius 1.3–1.9× with a 0.32–0.54 tilt squash — all by-eye aesthetic picks (`constants.js`), not measured against play. Tune if moons feel too fast/slow or rings too subtle/loud.
    - **Ring rendering (single pass, not Saturn-split)** — the ring arc draws as one pass behind the planet body (not front-and-back split around the sphere), for simplicity given "decorative only." Revisit only if a true Saturn-style occlusion look is wanted later.

OW-9 below is re-spec'd to not depend on this. Revisit only if the star map ends up feeling like it needs labels beyond Town.

### Wave 2 — redirected 2026-07-17: OW-9 shipped, OW-7/OW-8 parked

OW-9 below is re-spec'd to stand alone — it no longer needs OW-6's region names, OW-7's wormhole pairs, or OW-8's artifacts to be worth building. It shipped as **Town + fog-of-war discovery only**; if OW-7/OW-8 get built later, their discovered content can layer onto this same overlay as a follow-up, not a rebuild.

- [x] **P2 · OW-9 — Zoom-out star map + fog-of-war discovery tracking.** ✅ **Shipped 2026-07-17 (`92256eb`)** — a toggleable overlay (new 🗺️ Explore HUD button, next to ↺/☰) showing a wide zoomed-out view of the charted sector: **Town marked** at its location; **unvisited chunks render as fog/blank, chunks the comet has actually flown through get permanently revealed** (classic fog-of-war), persisted across reloads (`blackHoleInOne_discoveredChunks`). No region labels, no wormhole-pair markers, no artifact markers — those were OW-6/OW-7/OW-8's job and none of the three are built; shipped standalone as spec'd. **Purely informational, no gameplay effect** — the map pauses the run (reusing the existing `settingsOpened`/`settingsClosed` pause contract) and snapshot-renders once on open rather than looping every frame, since nothing can change while paused. See [Improvements](../ideas.md)'s Shipped section for the full architecture and in-browser verification trail. *Done when:* the map opens/closes without leaving the run ✅; Town renders at the correct relative position ✅; never-visited chunks stay fogged until visited, and that reveal persists across reload ✅; discovery-tracking logic is unit tested (+5 tests, 325 total) ✅; new keys registered in `clearAllGameData` ✅.
  - **OW-9 open sub-decisions (defaulted, non-blocking — revisit after playing, same convention as OW-3's/OW-5's):**
    - **Discovery is "flown through," not "visited."** `markDiscovered()` fires from `step()`/`stepOrbit()` only — never from `startRun()`, `completeWarp()`, or `useReturnPortal()`, all of which teleport the comet. A fresh save's very first star-map open shows only whatever chunk the comet has actually flown into since — Town's own starting chunk isn't pre-revealed just for materializing there. In practice this is nearly invisible (the first `step()` after leaving Town marks that chunk anyway), but if it reads as "the map should show a little charted circle around Town from the very start," the fix is a one-line `markDiscovered()` call added to `startRun()`.
    - **No pan/zoom, no tap-to-fly-there.** The map renders the whole bounded sector at once (19×19 chunks) — deliberately simple, matching "purely informational." Sprint 3's beacons + fast-travel list is the natural place for a tap-a-charted-chunk-to-set-a-waypoint interaction if the map ever needs to do more than inform.

**Parked 2026-07-17 (skipped for now, not declined — revisit after OW-9 ships):**

Un-scoped for now; if picked back up, still needs deliberate spawn clearance at the exit point (reset-and-drift vs. carry-through velocity — pick and document whichever reads best in testing).
- **OW-8 — Derelict / artifact loot.** A rarer, richer version of stardust: fixed-position collectible "artifacts" scattered sparsely and seeded per chunk.
