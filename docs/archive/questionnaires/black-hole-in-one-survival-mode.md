# Black Hole in One — Survival Mode Questionnaire

To shape the new Survival mode ("limited fuel, enemy mobs, fuel refreshes"), mark your preferences below by changing `[ ]` to `[x]`.

### Q1: How does fuel drain?
- [x] **Per-flick cost:** Fuel acts like a "max strokes" meter. Every flick costs a fixed amount.
- [ ] **Continuous burn:** Fuel drains continuously over time while you are flying, making speed essential.
- [ ] **Continuous burn + flick cost:** Both. Time is ticking, and pushing costs extra fuel.

### Q2: What are "enemy mobs"?
- [x] **Static hazards (Space Mines):** Stationary objects that instantly kill you or drain massive fuel if touched.
- [x] **Moving hazards (Asteroids/Comets):** Dumb projectiles that drift across the screen. You must dodge them.
- [ ] **Homing enemies (UFOs):** Enemies that actively steer towards you.
- [x] **Gravity traps:** Enemies that don't move much but have strong gravity wells that try to pull you in to your death.

### Q3: How is the world structured?
- [ ] **Endless procedural flight:** Like Explore mode, the world generates infinitely as you fly. You try to survive as long as possible (score = distance or time).
- [x] **Discrete levels:** Like Round mode, but you must reach the Black Hole before running out of fuel/health.
