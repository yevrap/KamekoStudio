**Ordered: the top is next.** The row order is the priority; the `#` is the item's number,
kept so reviews and decision records can refer to it. This is the main way to steer
without writing a word. Move a row up to make it happen sooner, delete it to drop it, or
add a row anywhere in plain words; the Product Owner refines it at the next `plan`. Your ordering is kept unless a
retro writes down why not.

**Ready** (sized, with acceptance criteria — `docs/studio/definition-of-ready.md`). The
epic and its budget are in [Direction](direction.md).

one per sprint). **Size:** S or M; an L is split before it's pulled.
