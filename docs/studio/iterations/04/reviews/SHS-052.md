## The finding, and why it is not fixed here

QA's nit is fixed by one assertion in `scripts/e2e.mjs`, a production file. Under this
same rule, that change could not be pushed until a review had seen it, and this was the
last review round. It is carried to the next iteration as the first production change to
go through the rule from the start.
