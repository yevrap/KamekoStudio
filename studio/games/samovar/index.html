Samovar

The evening is over

    What this is testing

    Samovar is the studio's first original game, and this is its core mechanic on its own. Tea is strong or weak by its ratio, not its amount: you pour the dark brew first, then top the cup up with hot water to the brim, so the one real decision is how long to pour the brew for this cup's size. Once the water goes in, the strength is fixed.

    It is fun if your misses shrink over an evening and you want to pour another. It is not if the cups feel random, or if the colour you're asked for can't be read at a glance. Stars and the best evening are all there is, on purpose: progression waits for a verdict on the pour.
