Samovar

The evening is over

    What this is testing

    Samovar is the studio's first original game, and this is its core mechanic, second pass. Tea is strong or weak by its ratio, not its amount: you pour the dark brew first, then top the cup up with hot water to the brim, so the one real decision is where to stop the brew. The glasses are shaped differently (a straight tea glass, a tulip glass, a wide bowl) and every one fills in the same time, so the right stop for one strength sits at a different height in each: about half-way up the straight glass for Amber, well under half-way in the tulip, two thirds of the way up the bowl.

    It is fun if your misses on the tulip and the bowl shrink over an evening as you learn where each one's stop sits, and you want to pour another. It is not if the shapes feel arbitrary, or the level can't be read in the tulip's narrow top. Stars and the best evening are all there is, on purpose: progression waits for a verdict on the pour.
